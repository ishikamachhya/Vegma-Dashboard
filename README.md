# Vegma-Dashboard
Built a dashboard to track sales performance, profitability, customer segments, and regional trends using interactive visualizations and KPI monitoring.

# Project Overview
The Vega Mart Sales Analytics & Forecasting Dashboard is a Business Intelligence solution developed in Power BI to analyze retail sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, profitability, shipping efficiency, and future sales trends. The dashboard provides a centralized view of key business metrics and enables stakeholders to monitor performance across products, regions, customer segments, payment methods, and states.

In addition to descriptive analytics, the project incorporates sales forecasting to predict future sales trends based on historical order data, helping business users make proactive inventory, marketing, and resource planning decisions.

# Business Problem
Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data from multiple products, customers, regions, and sales channels. However, without a centralized reporting system, decision-makers face challenges in:

Identifying high-performing products and customer segments.
Understanding regional sales performance.
Monitoring profitability and operational efficiency.
Tracking sales growth over time.
Predicting future demand and revenue.
Making informed inventory and marketing decisions.

The business needed an interactive dashboard that could transform raw sales data into meaningful insights while also providing sales forecasts to support future planning and business growth.

# Objective
1.Analyze overall sales performance and profitability.
2.Monitor key business KPIs in real time.
3.Identify top-performing products and regions.
4.Track customer purchasing behavior.
5.Evaluate shipping and payment preferences.
6.Compare yearly sales and profit performance.
7.Forecast future sales using historical trends.
8.Support data-driven decision-making through interactive visualizations.

# Forecasting Implementation
One of the key highlights of this project is the Forecasting Module.

The forecasting page uses historical sales data to predict future sales performance. Forecasting capabilities were applied to the time-series sales data to estimate likely future sales patterns.
